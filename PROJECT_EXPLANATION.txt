1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Virtual 3D Real Estate Walkthrough System" is an interactive, web-based
computer graphics application that bridges the gap between 2D architectural
blueprints and immersive 3D real estate walkthroughs.

The project is structured into five progressive architectural phases:
- Phase 1: Parametric single-room generation with exterior CAD OrbitControls.
- Phase 2: First-person walkthrough (FPS), analytical door/window wall cutouts,
           ceiling with interior lighting, and procedural canvas textures (hardwood/tile).
- Phase 3: Multi-room apartment floor plans (1BHK Contemporary, 2BHK Executive),
           third-person perspective (TPS) with articulated 3D visitor avatar,
           anti-wall-clip spring-arm camera, and instant [V] key perspective switching.
- Phase 4:
  1. Automated 2D Blueprint Image Parser & Extrusion:
     Upload any floor plan image (PNG, JPG, CAD export) or choose from built-in
     samples (Studio, 1BHK, Loft). An in-browser Computer Vision (CV) pipeline
     performs grayscale normalization, Otsu-style luminance thresholding,
     run-length wall segment detection, and instantaneous 3D mesh extrusion.
  2. Solar & Daylight Simulation Engine:
     A dynamic 24-hour time-of-day slider (06:00 to 22:00) with real-time astronomical
     sun elevation/azimuth calculations, rotating soft directional shadows, skybox
     color-temperature transitions (Dawn, Noon, Sunset, Night), and night interior lights.
- Phase 5:
  Interactive 3D Drag & Drop Furniture Staging System:
  A comprehensive interior design catalog featuring 8 categories and 80+ distinct
  3D procedural models (Living Room, Bedroom, Dining Room, Kitchen, Home Office,
  Entrance/Foyer, Bathroom, and Kids' Room). Users can drag furniture directly from
  the catalog onto the 3D floor using floor plane raycasting, or click "+ Add to Room".
  Placed items can be clicked in CAD Orbit view to attach an interactive Three.js
  TransformControls 3D gizmo (Move, Rotate, Scale, +90° rotation, Duplicate, and Delete).
  Staged furniture casts dynamic shadows from the solar engine, participates in avatar
  physical collision, and automatically persists in browser localStorage with JSON
  Export and Import capabilities.


2. TECHNOLOGIES & TOOLS USED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CORE 3D GRAPHICS & RENDERING:
   * Three.js (v0.186.0):
     - WebGL scene graph, shadow-mapped directional lighting (PCFShadowMap),
       materials (MeshStandardMaterial, MeshPhysicalMaterial), and custom geometry.
   * Camera & Manipulation Controls:
     - CAD OrbitControls with inertia damping, polar angle limits, and orthographic views.
     - First-Person (FPS) PointerLockControls with 60 FPS sliding wall collision physics.
     - Third-Person (TPS) over-the-shoulder chase camera with spring-arm collision avoidance.
     - TransformControls: Interactive 3D transformation gizmo (Translate, Rotate, Scale)
       with automatic floor clamping (Y = 0) and grid snapping (0.25m / 15°).
   * Procedural Humanoid Avatar (src/geometry/avatarGenerator.js):
     - Articulated 3D visitor model with shoulder/hip pivots and sinusoidal
       limb oscillations for realistic walking locomotion.
   * Procedural 3D Furniture Library (src/geometry/furnitureLibrary.js):
     - 80+ distinct furniture models across 8 room categories constructed using
       procedural Three.js geometries with PBR materials (walnut/oak woods, fabrics,
       brushed brass, marble, smoked glass, and chrome).
   * Solar Daylight Engine (src/components/Viewer3D.jsx):
     - Dynamic spherical sun/moon vectors, directional shadow cascades,
       hemisphere ambient light transitions, and point light intensification.

B. APPLICATION & INTERACTION LOGIC:
   * React (v19.2.8):
     - Real-time state management for staged furniture, transform modes, catalog drawer,
       and walkthrough navigation.
   * HTML5 Drag & Drop API & Raycasting:
     - Calculates mouse ray intersection against the 3D ground plane (Y = 0) to spawn
       furniture exactly under the cursor.
   * LocalStorage & JSON Serialization:
     - Auto-saves staged furniture layouts per floor plan with Export/Import JSON support.


3. KEY ALGORITHMS & MATHEMATICAL IMPLEMENTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DRAG & DROP FLOOR PLANE RAYCASTING:
   When an item is dragged from the catalog and dropped onto the 3D canvas:
   1. Convert browser client coordinates (e.clientX, e.clientY) into normalized
      device coordinates (NDC) in [-1, 1]:
        NDC_x = (clientX - rect.left) / rect.width * 2 - 1
        NDC_y = -((clientY - rect.top) / rect.height) * 2 + 1
   2. Cast a ray from camera through the viewport:
        raycaster.setFromCamera(new THREE.Vector2(NDC_x, NDC_y), camera)
   3. Intersect the ray with the infinite horizontal floor plane at Y = 0:
        Plane = THREE.Plane(normal = (0, 1, 0), constant = 0)
        hitPoint = ray.intersectPlane(Plane)
   4. Instantiate the procedural 3D model, set its position to (hitPoint.x, 0, hitPoint.z),
      and add it to the scene's furniture group.

B. TRANSFORM GIZMO & FLOOR CONSTRAINT:
   1. When TransformControls is in 'translate' mode, the furniture's Y position is
      clamped to Y = 0 on every 'change' event:
        mesh.position.y = 0
      This guarantees furniture never floats into mid-air or drops below the floor.
   2. When dragging the gizmo, OrbitControls is automatically disabled to prevent
      conflicting camera rotation:
        transformControls.addEventListener('dragging-changed', e => orbitControls.enabled = !e.value)

C. PHYSICAL COLLISION INJECTION:
   Every frame in the 60 FPS physics loop, bounding boxes (THREE.Box3) are calculated
   for all placed furniture items and appended to the room's obstacle colliders array.
   The player/avatar's bounding capsule is checked against these boxes, preventing the
   visitor from walking through placed sofas, tables, or beds!


4. KEY VIVA / PRESENTATION QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: What are all the navigation camera modes supported in the application?
A1: 1. CAD Orbit View: Exterior dollhouse inspection with 360-degree rotation, zoom,
       pan, and perspective/orthographic presets (Top, Front, Side).
    2. First-Person Walkthrough (FPS): Eye-level navigation (1.65m) through the eyes
       of the visitor with mouse-look and keyboard walking.
    3. Third-Person Walkthrough (TPS): Over-the-shoulder chase camera tracking an
       interactive 3D visitor avatar with anti-wall-clip spring-arm mechanics.

Q2: How does the 3D Drag & Drop Furniture Staging system work?
A2: When an item is dragged from the catalog drawer onto the WebGL canvas, HTML5 drag
    events pass the furniture type identifier. Three.js Raycaster calculates the exact
    intersection between the camera ray and the floor plane at Y = 0. The procedural
    model is instantiated with PBR materials, added to the scene, and automatically
    selected with a Three.js TransformControls 3D gizmo.

Q3: Why are procedural 3D geometries used instead of downloading 80+ external GLTF files?
A3: Procedural modeling eliminates network latency, CORS security errors, broken external
    links, and massive multi-megabyte downloads. All 80+ furniture models generate in
    microseconds directly in memory, share cached PBR materials, run completely offline,
    and guarantee consistent architectural scale and floor grounding (Y = 0).

Q4: What actions can the user perform on staged furniture?
A4: 1. Move across the floor plane (G key).
    2. Rotate around the vertical Y-axis (R key or +90° quick snap button).
    3. Scale dimensions (S key).
    4. Duplicate with an offset (Duplicate button).
    5. Delete individual items (Delete key / button) or Clear All.
    6. Export and Import the entire staged interior layout as a JSON file.
    7. Auto-save layout in browser localStorage.

Q5: Do staged furniture items cast shadows and block avatar movement?
A5: Yes. Every furniture mesh enables castShadow and receiveShadow, so shadows dynamically
    rotate around furniture as the sun moves across the sky. Additionally, the physics
    engine injects furniture Box3 bounding boxes into the collision detector, preventing
    the player avatar from walking through furniture during tours.
